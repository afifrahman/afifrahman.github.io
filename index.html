About Me

I am a Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate student at BRAC University, passionate about leveraging technology to solve real-world problems. My academic journey has been driven by a deep curiosity for research and innovation in computer science.

I am particularly interested in areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, software engineering, and human-computer interaction.
